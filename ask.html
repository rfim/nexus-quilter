How I work

Let them ask

The best report is the one nobody has to request. First, I build the semantic layer: find the competing definitions, agree one with its owner, write it as code, test it and publish it. Then people can just ask, and every answer runs on the live lakehouse as them, with the source cited.

What the job description asks: “Enable self-service analytics through well-designed data products and semantic layers” and “Explore and apply AI capabilities responsibly.” At Lifepal, putting definitions into the semantic layer first cut ad-hoc requests by 60%.
