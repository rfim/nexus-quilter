      05 · Team and deliveryThe First Light playbook
      
    
      
      
      First Light: working under pressure
      
      
      My playbook for uncertain requirements, urgent demand or a short-staffed team: deliver the smallest piece of truth that helps today's decision, keep it reversible, write down the debt, and protect the team's focus. In short: ship the candle, build the lighthouse later.
      
    
      
    
      
      
      1
      Light over perfection
      Perfect architecture delivered late illuminates nothing. Take the simplest reversible path that gives clarity today.
      
      
      2
      Signal over noise
      Filter every request by the decision it enables today. What single decision does this data need to support?
      
      
      3
      Sanctuary for execution
      Shield the team from ad hoc chaos and context switching. One clear focus produces real velocity.
      
    
      
    
      
      
      
        
      Try the triage
      
        
      Pick a request. It lands where the playbook puts it, with the reason why.
      
        
        
      
        
      
        
        
        Urgent · importantDo first
        Critical path and minimum viable truth only.
        
        
        Important · not urgentSchedule
        Modelling, governance, debt logged during First Light.
        
        
        Urgent · not importantDelegate
        Certified metric views and the governed agent.
        
        
        NeitherEliminate
        No report without a decision attached.

        06 · First 90 daysLearning the domain first
        
    
        My first 90 days
        
    
        
      
        Days 1–30
        Listen and inventory
        Learn the domain from its owners and its data. Build a queryable record of what exists, what it feeds and who owns it, from catalog lineage. Change nothing.
        
      
        Days 31–60
        Own
        Take the highest-traffic products first. Run them, fix them and join their incident rota. Write contracts for the rules that currently live in someone's head.
        
      
        Days 61–90
        Standardise
        Agree one ingestion pattern, one quality mechanism and one deployment path with the other leads, adopted where each has proved itself twice.
        
    
        
  
        

        


        
  
        
    
        
      
        07 · What is realSources
        
      
        What is real and what is synthetic
        
    
        
    
        
      
        Real: the Lifepal outcomes in section 01, from my CV and production work. The PySpark pipeline, contracts, compiler and tests in this repository, which run end to end with ./scripts/build.sh and in CI.
        
      
        Synthetic: every client, colleague, fund and transaction. They are generated by src/nexus/generate.py, including replayed records, malformed rows and one ledger break, so the controls have real work to do. No Quilter or Lifepal data is used.
        
      
        Depth: in production I have run Databricks within Azure (Delta on ADLS Gen2, Workflows, ADF, Key Vault, Spark tuning, Kafka and Debezium CDC). I know Unity Catalog, Asset Bundles and Lakeflow declarative pipelines at working level, and Data Vault and Fabric at concept level.
