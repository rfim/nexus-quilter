ELT end to end

Your browser fetches the raw landing files, loads them into bronze with MERGE, splits bad rows into quarantine, builds silver and gold, checks every department's contract, then takes a late batch and restates. Every step is real SQL in DuckDB-WASM; click a task to see what it ran. With the default scenarios, the counts should match the Spark run.
